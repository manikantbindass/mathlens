# Community conduct

Treat contributors and learners with respect. Welcome different mathematical backgrounds and cultures. Critique ideas and reproducible evidence, not people. Do not harass, discriminate, publish private information or misrepresent research claims.

Maintainers may moderate disruptive content and should explain decisions fairly. A private reporting channel and enforcement contacts must be selected by the repository owner before community launch. No unverified contact address or response promise is supplied here.
